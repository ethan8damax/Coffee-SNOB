# Coffee Snob

Turborepo monorepo: `apps/web` (Next.js marketing site) + `apps/app` (Expo
product app), sharing Supabase as the backend.

Brand voice and audience are locked in `apps/web/PRODUCT.md` — read it
before writing or editing any user-facing copy in either app, not just
`apps/web`. Design tokens/system: `apps/web/DESIGN.md`. Shop vetting
standards: `apps/web/CURATION-STANDARDS.md`. How to write a shop write-up:
`apps/web/WRITEUP-GUIDE.md`. Publishing states and moderation rules:
`apps/web/CONTENT-OPS.md`. Monetization rules (what can carry commercial
links, and where): `MONETIZATION.md` — check it before adding any affiliate
link, ad, or paid placement anywhere in this repo.

Design/spec history: `docs/superpowers/specs/`. Implementation plans:
`docs/superpowers/plans/`.

## Launch tracking

The v1 soft-launch plan is tracked in `docs/v1-launch-tracker.md`. Check items
off and add a dated Status log line as work lands.
